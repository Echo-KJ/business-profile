Modular prefab construction takes place in a highly controlled factory setting, ensuring precision, less material waste, and zero weather delays. Units are then transported to site. On-site construction is traditional, subject to weather conditions, and takes significantly longer.
Yes, depending on your land documentation and the banking partner, you can often secure a home loan or a personal construction loan. We assist with required structure documentation for the bank.
In many cases, because these are temporary, relocatable structures without deep foundations, they bypass lengthy traditional permit processes. However, local panchayat or municipal rules vary, so consulting local authorities is recommended.
Prefab cabins require minimal foundations. Usually, a few concrete pads, pillars, or a simple ground screw system is sufficient, reducing environmental impact and site preparation costs drastically.
